# miPharmacy

**Partner type:** retail network partner (pharmacy channel). **Industry:** pharmacy.

miPharmacy is Personalisation Hub's pharmacy channel partner. Personalisation Hub provides the platform; miPharmacy brings it to its community pharmacy network and runs brand sponsorship with pharma brands. This page sets out how the two brands appear together, so every co-branded piece looks and reads the same.

The general Brand Guidelines still apply to everything here: logo usage, colour, typography, imagery and the founder tone of voice. This page only adds what changes when miPharmacy is on the page too.

## Who leads, by audience

The audience decides which brand leads. Pick the row first, then follow it for the lockup, colours and wording.

| Audience | Lead brand | Line under the lead logo | Typical pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pharma brands and advertisers | miPharmacy | "Powered by Personalisation Hub" | Brand prospectus, sponsorship tiers, pilot reports |
| Pharmacy owners | Co-branded (equal) | "miPharmacy and Personalisation Hub" | Owner one-pager, USP cards, onboarding guides |
| PH sales, enterprise and AWS conversations | Personalisation Hub | "In partnership with miPharmacy" | Platform overview, decks, AWS-track material |

## Co-brand logo lockups

Always use the official logo files. The Personalisation Hub files are in Brand assets; the miPharmacy files are added to Brand assets once miPharmacy supplies them, and the lockups above pick them up from there. Never redraw, recolour or retype either logo.

- **Order:** the lead brand sits on the left (or on top, when stacked). On co-branded pieces for pharmacy owners, miPharmacy sits on the left.
- **Divider:** a 1px vertical hairline between the two logos, the height of the taller logo's mark. Wordmark grey `#7B7B7B` on light surfaces, white at 40% opacity on dark surfaces.
- **Spacing:** the gap from each logo to the divider equals the height of one bar of the PH mark.
- **Size:** both logos are optically the same height. The supporting brand is never larger than the lead brand.
- **Clear space:** at least one bar's height on every side of the whole lockup. Nothing else sits inside it.
- **Light and dark:** on light surfaces use the PH full colour logo and miPharmacy's full colour logo. On dark surfaces use the PH white logo and miPharmacy's reversed (white) logo, with no chip or box behind either.
- **Placement:** the lockup takes the logo's usual home, top-left. Never add a second PH logo elsewhere on the same page or slide.
- **Mono:** for print, watermarks and small sizes, both logos in mono.

## Colour hierarchy

- **miPharmacy leads** (pharma brands and advertisers): miPharmacy's own palette carries the piece. Personalisation Hub appears only in the "Powered by" lockup, with no PH accent colours.
- **Co-branded** (pharmacy owners): Personalisation Hub's light surface and typography, with **teal** `#169BC2` as the one accent. miPharmacy's colour appears only in its logo.
- **Personalisation Hub leads** (PH sales, enterprise and AWS): the standard PH brand. miPharmacy appears only in its logo and the "In partnership with" line.
- Never mix the two palettes in one element, and never use all four PH bar colours outside the PH logo mark.

## Naming, wording and tone

- Write the partner's name exactly as **miPharmacy**: lower-case "mi", capital "P", one word.
- Write **Personalisation Hub** in full on first mention, spelt with an S. Never "PH" in customer-facing copy.
- Approved lines: "Powered by Personalisation Hub" (miPharmacy leads), "In partnership with miPharmacy" (PH leads), "miPharmacy and Personalisation Hub" (co-branded).
- Tone: the founder tone of voice, plain and practical. For pharmacy owners, plain-language benefits a busy owner can read in a minute; for pharma brands, outcomes and proof.
- Screens shown in images always show content. SMS is used only for queuing notifications, never for promotions.

## Approved claims and outcomes

The pharmacy industry page on the website is the single source of truth for the pharmacy story: [personalisationhub.com/industries/pharmacy](https://personalisationhub.com/industries/pharmacy/). Use its wording; don't restate it here.

The outcomes every co-branded piece speaks to:

- Services and consultations booked.
- Script conversion.
- A bigger front-of-shop basket.
- Retail media revenue for the pharmacy.

For pharmacy owners, the six benefits are:

1. **Zero-cost entry:** screens funded by pharma brands.
2. **Walk-ins for funded services:** an external display that brings people in for government-funded services.
3. **Proof it works:** a closed-loop attribution dashboard.
4. **One view of the patient:** a unified patient profile at the point of care.
5. **A share of the advertising:** the pharmacy's revenue share on advertising.
6. **Compete with the big chains:** a competitive defence against the major chains.

Statistics need a source before they reach a customer.
