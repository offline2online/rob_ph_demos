# Product Hunt: Run Your Own RMN launch

Gallery images for the Product Hunt launch of Run Your Own RMN under the existing
Personalisation Hub product (draft on Rob's account, 16 Nov 2026). Each is 1270×760 at 2x
(2540×1520), dark surface, one accent each, the official white logo, and a real product screen
from the golf club tour (`ph-website/tour-assets/rmn-golf/v2`, demo data from the fictional
Fairlinks Golf Network).

| File | Headline | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| `ph-gallery-1.png` | Bring venues into your network. | Stores / Branches |
| `ph-gallery-2.png` | Sell screen time to brands. | Advertisers / Inventory |
| `ph-gallery-3.png` | Keep slots for venue promotions. | Playlist slot assignment |
| `ph-gallery-4.png` | One schedule for every venue. | Advertiser booking schedule |

The revenue and booking-value screens are left out on purpose: their demo figures would read as
earnings claims, and the Run Your Own RMN page never states any.

Product Hunt fetches these images by their commit URL on raw.githubusercontent.com.
This sandbox can't reach Product Hunt's upload host.
