Language Registry

Company-level reference data for the Languages On Shift display item. The store record only ever sends a language identifier (an ISO code, an English name, or an endonym) — everything needed to render it, the flag, the native-script name, reading direction and script, comes from this table. A language a store's record can send but that isn't listed here simply won't appear on screen.

A flag represents a country, not a language. English, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, French and Chinese are each spoken across many countries, and a single national flag can read as excluding other speakers of that language. Either pick the flag most recognisable to a given store's local population, or turn Show Flag off and use a glyph mark instead (for example 🤟 for British Sign Language).
