# Products, Pricing & Assets — code review, 19 Oct 2026

A read-only review of the whole Products, Pricing & Assets prototype as it
stands on `main` at `085e4b5`: `hq-admin.html`, the per-product editor
(`product-app/` and its build in `product/`), `retail-admin.html`,
`menu-board.html`, the Cloud Functions in `functions/`, the root
`firestore.rules`, the portal and support pages, and the older
`Brands-Suppliers-Assets/` prototype. Nothing was changed by the review
itself; fixes are left as backlog work on the board project **Products and
Pricing Prototype** (`products-and-pricing`).

Line numbers are as of `085e4b5`. Severity: **High** = data loss, money or
credentials at risk, or the customer-facing price is wrong; **Medium** = a
visible bug or a real cost; **Low** = polish, hygiene or a narrow edge.

## Summary

The prototype is feature-rich and the product editor is well built. Four
things matter before any real client data or credentials go into it:

1. **There is no access control anywhere.** The Firestore rules let anyone
   delete the catalogue and rewrite every setting, and the AI Cloud
   Functions accept unauthenticated calls — including one that will send
   the stored, server-encrypted provider keys to any URL the caller saves.
2. **The PH API keys are only obfuscated.** The decryption passphrase ships
   in the page source and the settings document is publicly readable.
3. **Store-level pricing freezes HQ.** Every Retail Admin pricing save also
   saves the RRP and copies HQ's current offer as the store's own, so later
   HQ price and offer changes never reach that store.
4. **Scheduled and recurring offers are not reliable.** The board never
   re-renders on a timer, the expiry sweep wipes recurring offers after
   their first day, and the three pages each carry a hand-copied, already
   drifting copy of the price-resolution logic.

console's `auth-gate.js` / `consoleUsers` pattern, or App Check at least),
close the `menuboard`, `items` delete and store-collection rules, and move
the PH API call behind a Cloud Function proxy like the AI providers already
use, so no client ever holds a PH token. Lock `baseUrl` to an allowlist per
provider kind, or re-require the token whenever `baseUrl` changes.

## 2. Pricing and offers — correctness

How a price is chosen is implemented three times by hand
(`_resolveItemOffersForStore` + `getItems()` in `menu-board.html:1327-1382`,
`_resolveItemOffersForStore` + `_effectivePricing` in
`retail-admin.html:1283-1331`, the flat fields + `_isOfferWindowLiveNow` in
`hq-admin.html:3520`), plus `pickDefaultOffer` / `getOfferState` in
product-app and the sweep in `functions/`. They have already drifted.

| # | Sev | Where | Defect and failure |
|---|-----|-------|--------------------|
| P1 | High | `retail-admin.html:2655` | Every store pricing save writes `price: rrpNum`, even when only the offer or note changed. HQ later raises the RRP and that store keeps the old one forever — on the board too. A blank RRP field saves `price: 0` and the board shows £0.00. |
| P2 | High | `retail-admin.html:2369, 2651-2661` | The pricing form opens pre-filled from `_effectivePricing`, which falls back to HQ's resolved offer, so saving *any* change writes HQ's offer into `storePricing` as the store's own. When HQ ends or reprices the promotion that store keeps the old one (store `offerPrice > 0` wins). A copied recurring HQ offer becomes one flat window and expires after day one. |
| P3 | High | `menu-board.html:2045, 2361` | The board renders only on a Firestore change, a PH API poll result or a page rotation — never on a timer. A single-page or `?screen=N` board never switches an offer on at its start time, and recurring lunch-time offers never turn on or off. Expiry is up to 15 min late and only when the sweep happens to write. Retail's grid has the same gap. |
| P4 | High | `functions/index.js:77-91` | `sweepExpiredOffers` treats `offerUntil` as a final end date, but for a recurring offer it is the *daily* end time (`recurrence.js:12-15`). A "Daily 11:00–14:00" offer is wiped from the flat fields after 14:00 on day one, a false "Offer expired… reverted to RRP" goes into `priceLog`, `offerRecurrence` is left orphaned, and `offers[]` is untouched — so the HQ grid says "No offer" while the boards still show it, and the next Pricing save brings it back to be wiped again. |
| P5 | Medium | `hq-admin.html:3520-3522` | `_isOfferWindowLiveNow` returns `true` whenever `offerFrom` is empty without checking `offerUntil`. An offer with only "until yesterday" shows struck-through in the grid and passes "Offer running" while the product page says it ended. |
| P6 | Medium | `menu-board.html:1377`, `retail-admin.html:1331` | A store offer beats HQ on `offerPrice > 0` alone, without being live. A store schedules next week's deal and HQ's live national offer disappears at that store now. |
| P7 | Medium | `menu-board.html:1336, 1452` | An ended or future offer can win resolution; the item then has `offerPrice > 0` but is not live, and `shouldShowMenuNote` suppresses the standing `menuBoardNote` too. "Fresh daily" vanishes for good once any offer has ended. |
| P8 | Medium | `retail-admin.html:1168` vs `menu-board.html:1226` | Retail's `_offerTargetingMatches` drops per-group schedules, treats an empty group as "no match" (the board: "match everything") and ignores `queueing`. Retail says an offer is live all week that the board only runs at the weekend. |
| P9 | Medium | `menu-board.html:1309` | Recurring windows are read with `getHours()` in the *viewing device's* timezone; the parsed `store_timezone` is never used. HQ, Retail and the board can disagree on whether an offer is live. |
| P10 | Medium | `menu-board.html` `getItems()`; `retail-admin.html:2075` | `allowStorePricing` is only checked to show Retail's tab. Switch it off for a brand and existing store prices keep showing on boards while stores can no longer see or clear them. Distribution is also inconsistent: Retail filters by legacy `brand.stores`, the board ignores both that and `distributionTargeting`. |
| P11 | Medium | `retail-admin.html:2254, 2279, 2303` | Changing the offer's start always resets its end to start + 1 h, silently shortening a two-week offer. An end date picked with no time stores 00:00, ending the offer at the *start* of that day. |
| P12 | Low | `hq-admin.html:1897` | "Return to RRP" leaves `offerPrice == RRP` with no dates, which counts as a live local offer: a permanent "Local offer" pill in HQ and HQ offers suppressed at that store. Confirm whether that suppression is intended. |
| P13 | Low | `retail-admin.html` Price column | Sorts by HQ's price, not the store's effective price it displays. |

Fix direction: one shared, tested price-resolution module (pure JS, no
Firebase) imported by product-app and loaded by the three pages and the
sweep; store overrides as per-field patches (RRP only if the store changed
it, offer only if the store set one); a 30–60 s re-render tick on the board
and Retail grid; a sweep that understands `offerRecurrence` / `recurrence.ends`
and re-picks the flat fields from `offers[]`; recurring windows in the
store's timezone.

## 4. Accessibility and the PH design system

- **`position: fixed` inside iframed admin pages** (contrary to
  `ph-designer` → `references/prototyping.md`): hq-admin modals, confirm,
  toasts, overlays, row menu (`hq-admin.html:246, 332, 341, 529, 2993,
  3052`); Retail Admin toasts, modals and time picker
  (`retail-admin.html:164, 205, 394, 510`). In a tall iframe these land
  outside the visible area. product-app is clean (`sticky` only).
- **No ARIA in hq-admin or retail-admin**: no `role="dialog"`, focus trap,
  Escape-to-close or labels on icon-only buttons; tabs, row menus, stars and
  stock pills are `<div onclick>`. product-app's asset tiles
  (`ProductAssetsTab.jsx:446`) and the offer delete button
  (`PricingTab.jsx:~496`) have the same gaps.
- **Fonts / icons**: hq-admin, retail-admin, the portal and product-app
  correctly use Roboto + Material Symbols Outlined. product-app uses a
  `ui-monospace` stack for SKUs and store codes in five places, and loads
  Tailwind's base reset alongside antd. hq-admin has emoji placeholders
  (`3616, 3619`). The customer-facing boards (menu-board, store-hours,
  languages-on-shift, order pages) use Inter / Oswald — fine for signage,
  now documented as a deliberate exception in `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
  `api-reference.html` (Inter/JetBrains Mono) and `location-api.html`
  (system stack) deviate.
- **`Brands-Suppliers-Assets/`** predates the design system: system font
  stack, Google *Material Icons* (not Symbols), emoji navigation and its own
  sidebar/topbar. It is a legacy prototype (see `README.md`); don't extend it.

## 5. Build, deploy and hygiene

- **`product/` is a checked-in build of `product-app/`** (Vite, `outDir:
  '../product'`). It is current (source and bundle both last changed in
  `b68e4526`, 2 Sep 2026), but **nothing rebuilds it** — every source change
  needs `npm run build` and a commit of the output, and a ticket's change is
  invisible on GitHub Pages until then. `dsp-prototype.yml` is the pattern to
  copy if this becomes frequent. The build is 26 MB, mostly ONNX runtime.
- **`functions/` and `firestore.rules` deploy by hand** (`firebase deploy
  --only functions` / `--only firestore:rules` against `rob-ph-demos`). Whether
  the deployed versions match the repo is unknown from here. No Storage rules
  are in the repo (`generated-videos/` lives in that bucket). Functions run on
  Node 20 — check Google's runtime deprecation dates before the next deploy.
- **`@imgly/background-removal`** is AGPL-3.0 with a commercial licence
  option — check before any client-facing use.
- **Dead code**: hq-admin's `exportData` / `importData` / `clearAllData`
  (`6016-6250`, no UI caller), `_ensureTemplates` (`5091`), an unused live
  listener on the whole `stock` collection (`6515-6518`), `currSymbol`
  (`6459`), and the old client-side AI-token encryption (`2080-2110`). The
  hidden Languages On Shift preview iframe (`1247`) boots a second Firebase
  app on every load. The hint at `5069` names tabs that don't exist.
- **Unreferenced files**: `menu-board.zip`, the Pizza Hut PDF,
  `images/los-*.png` (~2.1 MB), `images/icons/cart.svg`, `location-api.html`
  (no page links to it). `serve.py` and `.claude/launch.json` reference paths
  that only exist on one laptop.
- **No tests.** The pure pieces (price resolution, `recurrence.js`,
  `migration.js`, Magic Edit's maths) are the obvious first ones.

## 6. Recommended order of work

1. **Close the doors (S1–S4)** before any real token or client catalogue
   goes in: rotate the AI and PH keys afterwards, since they must be assumed
   exposed.
2. **One price-resolution module with tests (P1–P11)**, then a board
   re-render tick and a recurrence-aware sweep.
3. **Targeted writes (D1, D2, D4, D13)**: `updateDoc` of changed fields and
   `arrayUnion` for logs instead of whole-document and whole-catalogue sets.
4. **Move images and brand files to Storage (D5, D7)** and lazy-load the
   background-removal model (D6).
5. **Escape the remaining `innerHTML` sinks (S5)** and replace
   `position: fixed` overlays in the admin pages.
6. **Housekeeping**: remove the zip/PDF/unused images, dead code, and decide
   whether `Brands-Suppliers-Assets/` should be retired.
